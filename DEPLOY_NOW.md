# 🚀 Deploy Your Portfolio in 5 Minutes

Choose one of these three super-fast deployment methods:

## Method 1: 📱 Netlify (Easiest - 2 minutes)

1. **Go to [netlify.com](https://netlify.com)**
2. **Sign up for free** (use GitHub account for easier setup)
3. **Drag & drop** your entire project folder to the deploy area
4. **Done!** Your site is live instantly

**✅ Your URL:** `https://random-name-123456.netlify.app`

**🎯 Pro tip:** Your contact form will work automatically on Netlify!

---

## Method 2: 🐱 GitHub Pages (Free forever)

1. **Create GitHub account** at [github.com](https://github.com)
2. **Create new repository** named `your-username.github.io`
3. **Upload files:** Drag `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js` into the repo
4. **Go to Settings** → **Pages** → **Deploy from main branch**
5. **Done!** Live at `https://your-username.github.io`

---

## Method 3: ⚡ Vercel (Developer favorite)

1. **Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com)**
2. **Sign up with GitHub**
3. **Import your repository**
4. **Deploy automatically**
5. **Done!** Live at `https://your-portfolio.vercel.app`

---

## 🎯 After Deployment:

1. **Test your site** on mobile and desktop
2. **Share your URL** with friends and recruiters
3. **Update your resume** with the portfolio link
4. **Add to LinkedIn** profile

## 🔄 Making Updates:

**Netlify:** Drag new files to redeploy  
**GitHub Pages:** Edit files in GitHub repository  
**Vercel:** Push to GitHub repository  

---

## ⚡ Quick Customization:

Before deploying, quickly update these in `index.html`:

```html
<!-- Line 36: Your name -->
<span class="highlight">Your Actual Name</span>

<!-- Line 37: Your title -->
<p class="hero-subtitle">Your Job Title</p>

<!-- Line 296: Your email -->
<span>your.actual.email@example.com</span>
```

**🎉 That's it! Your portfolio is now live on the web!**
